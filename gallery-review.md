## Exclusions and uncertain groupings

- Some photographs are visually similar and may be alternative views of the same item, but without a clear label or matching product context they were not silently merged.
- The four combed leaf wall hangings (lake blue, lavender, natural, rust) and the two multi-feather wall hangings (blue, pink) appear to be the same handmade design repeated in different colourways, similar to the existing blue/colour gradient wall hangings. Each is listed as its own gallery item since every photo is a genuine finished piece, and this supports the gallery page's existing message that colours can be discussed for custom commissions.
- The "poppy dog lead" and "money pouch" did not fit any of the five existing categories, so they were placed in "Other creations" alongside coasters and the owls (practical/character pieces rather than wall decor, plant hangers, keyrings or bookmarks).
- "Olive-eyed owl" (source file `owl.jpeg`) was checked against the existing "Feathered owl" (source file `feathured_owl.jpeg`) to confirm they are two different pieces, not duplicate photos of the same item — the existing one is a fluffy grey/white roving owl, the new one is a cream macramé owl with olive-green eyes on a brass ring. Both are kept as separate gallery items.
- "Miniature framed macramé" is a small sample piece shown on a display easel rather than hung on a wall, so it was placed in "Other creations" rather than "Wall hangings" alongside the existing framed wall hanging (which is designed to hang).
- Some files such as the logo and a few other utility images were excluded from the gallery inventory because they are not product photographs.
- The source folder includes a mixture of product images and unrelated files. The gallery catalogue keeps to handmade work only.
- The file naming is helpful but not definitive — for example "coral_wall_hanging.jpeg" is mustard, teal and cream, not coral coloured, so it was renamed to "Mustard and teal flower wall hanging" on the site. Titles generally are based on the visible content of each image rather than any implied product name.
- "celtic_syle_bookmarks.jpeg" was originally catalogued as "Celtic style bookmarks" in Bookmarks, based on the source filename. The business owner confirmed the pictured items are actually keyrings, so it was renamed to "Celtic style keyrings" and moved to Keyrings and wristlets.
